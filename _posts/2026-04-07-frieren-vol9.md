# Frieren: Vol 7 - 9
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/85051185602)

This is basically covering Vol 7 to 9. Frieren finishes the laybrinth, gets insulted by the other elf mistress and then continues her quest. I'll be honest and say at this point i'm not really sure what the quest is anymore, since its been a while since i started reading. I'm sure its more of the "defeat big bad demon and blah blah blah", and at this point just treating this series as yet another slice of life of demon slayer/adventurers.

There's still a little bit of "the difference between long lived elves and short lived humans" but the focus has firmly moved on to the big bad of the week and maybe some movement of the characters. In particular the fighter and the apprentice mage has now moved to the will-they/will-they-not territory and starting to become a bit annoying. I really hope they pickup a cleric and maybe a thief soon since those are the missing archetypes, though I guess with Frieren they don't really need a thief. A cleric is a must though.

In any case, I'll update the series as I read through it. At some point I'll have to start from the beginning since I only read this when I'm really out of reading material. Once again, I don't begrudge authors for milking a series, but you really come to appreciate the Jun Mayuzuki's who can write and finish a series without milking it. After the Rain, even 3 years after reading it is still one of the strongest manga's i've read.

Oh well, onward! Very mild recommend if you're interested, but start in the beginning.
