# Children of Strife
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8452462342)

Another year, another Children of book. This time we're transported yet another few (or few hundred) years into the future from the last book. I have enjoyed every single one of the Children of books, but the first 2 still had the strongest impression on me. The last one I only remember its of Ravens and a play on Hugin and Munin, but not much of the plot is impressed on me.

This one is better I think, in that there are strong characters, not just concepts. You have the VERY ANGRY Mantis Shrimp pilot, also of the world where the Spiders came from. You don't hear about them mostly becuase they were happy to be of the Ocean while the Spiders took to the stars. They only went on to the Stars after the Spiders introduced them to the concept of stars and stuff. Anyway, that's one character.

You have the biological computer of the late scientist who was responsible for the spiders being uplifted, you have the all-consuming entity of the second book, who can become anything and anyone but has to eat them before they can become that someone/something...you have humans as well, and a brand new world where terraforming/uplifting has gone wild.

Amidst all that, you have the curious question of why this rag tag band of adventurers are out there by themselves, with no support.

You also get a glimpse of what happened on Earth to cause the mass blackout and incommunicative status of the entire galaxy from the cradle of human civilization. There are egos, and insanity and a lot of backstabbing and academic politics mixed in with not a small amount of body horror. All mixed in with the usual Tchaikovsky humor of course.

There's, as usual, a happy ending, and a lot of wrap up with the possibility of more Children of books to come. I want to say that there are some new ideas here that wasn't already explored in Alien Clay or one of the other novels, but I think you just have to take this book on face value and that its an exploration of a new world by new characters and there's a lot of cool action scenes and not much advancement of any huge philosophies. I wouldn't classify it exactly as brain candy, there is some thought processes to be had, and more so than Children of Memory, but once again, nothing as imaginative or impressive as Children of Time or Children of Ruin.

Still highly recommended!
