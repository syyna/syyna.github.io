# Automatic Noodle
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/9064130035)

This was a book that was recommended to me by Libby and although I don't really regret finishing it, it was solely because it was a very short book.

The premise is that robots are now sentient and have sort of gone through their civil rights moment. They have rights, but not acceptance so humans are still bigoted against them. They are used to do all the bad things that humans don't want to do (mine detection, body cremating/burying, etc), and are capable of developing PTSD now too.

The problem with this is that the robots are basically humans, with just swappable body parts. There's no...real robot about any of the characters. They make a restaurant, they create great chinese style noodles, and they come across some small business issues (bad reviews, review bombing, etc), and they resolve it.

there are some conflicts, but honestly, the book ran out its course the moment you realize you're just reading about humans in a robot skin.

Not recommended.
