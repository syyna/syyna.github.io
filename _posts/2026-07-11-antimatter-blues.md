# Antimatter Blues
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8762458130)

The last of the books that I read while on vacation. This is pretty much a direct sequel to Mickey 7 and as with all sequels, falls more than a little flat. There's no more interesting conceit now that Mickey is no longer the sacrificial scapegoat and is just a regular colonist. There's a new crisis regarding the bombs that Mickey was supposed to use the last book to blow up the aliens. The colony used fuel to create those bombs and now really would like those bombs back so they can reuse the fuel.

He has to go negotiate with the aliens from the last book and finds out that they aren't the only alien group on the planet. There's yet another alien group across the mountains and they gave those bombs over to them. Now Mickey and a select pick of crew has to go and negotiate the return of those fuel bombs.

The book and plot itself is pretty decent and it moves fast, just that the conceit is mostly gone now. this is now a fairly run of the mill sci fi adventure story (mostly an adventure story, with the science all pretty much sci fi adventure science). Its not badly written and you have to consume it like mind candy. If you look at it beyond the characters with their own in fightings and mostly snarky love of one another, there's not much more to it.

The aliens are kind of interesting, but not much than your typical alien insects stuff you've seen before. Still, the whole book is pretty satisfying as a conclusion touchstone to the whole mickey saga.
