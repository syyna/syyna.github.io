# Gate of Feral Gods
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8968886771)

This book introduces the dopplegangers, beings who can impersonate both physically and ability-wise anyone that they've seen before . They're a pretty common enemy type in D&D adventures so its not completely surprising to see them here. This book also introduces multiple quests for the different parties, where each group from the previous train group is now in their own separated environment (partially to prevent the type of steam roll a united front group can do), and mostly to whittle down the participants.

The heroes we know and love are stuck in some sort of weird desert envionrment where there are sentient camels defending their land against airship flying gnomes. There's some sort of weird detente between the two parties, and its up to the heroes to figure out. There's a series of quests/steps they have to do to break out of their environment and trigger the exit and most of the book is about that.

This is one of the weakest books I've read in the series so far, and I'm not sure exactly why. Its still pretty fast paced and there's still a lot of interplay between real world and dungeon world, but something about the pacing or the lack of purpose fills this one for some odd reason.

In any case, if you've gotten to book 4 i think its pretty telling that you're committed, so might as well slog through it and hope the next one gets better!
