# Penric and the Demonic Ox
[Goodreads Review] (https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8421304279)

Once again, catching up on the rest of the Penric and Desdemona series that I've missed. This one follows Penric in catching up with a demonic infestation of a local ox and the adventures that must follow when one deals with such an endeavor.

I'd say that the whole affair with the Ox is a bit of a McGuffin and the real story is how Penric deals with his daughter's desire to follow in his shamanic footsteps. Not just his daughter, but also all the charges that he has responsbility for. So his adopted daughter, his blood daughter, and a son. How his fatherhood affects his own godly and not-so-godlly duties is basically what I consider the meat of this episode of Penric. Its incredibly refreshing to see how in a series a character grows, and in this case, we've seen Penric grow from a feckless 19 year old to a responsible father somewhere in his mid 30s or early 40s.

Very few series manages to do that becuase of plot or other reasons and though this can be seen sort of as a "slice-of-life" with some "life-in-danger" subevents, I actually appreciate this type of character growth now that I'm older too.

In any case, highly recommended.
