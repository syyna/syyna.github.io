# Mickey7
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8748613332)

This was one of those books that showed up as an easy read on my list and I definitely needed an easy read after a 14 hour plane ride.

Its science fiction in the sense that it talks about clones and what its like to be a clone who's asked to do the worst things (drug testing, vaccine testing, go clean out radioactive waste), and the reasons to do so is mostly spurrious (there's 0 way getting a robot to clean out radioactive waste is more expensive than cloning a human), but as a thought experiment on how one clone might react to memories of another clone, its interesting.

There is a bit of a story, they're trying to colonize a new planet, and they met up with these alien creatures called critters. A fall happens and rather than try to rescue the clone, the pilot decided to leave him to die. He doesn't die and ends up meeting the new clone. Shenanigans ensue and the rest of the book is about how to survive as this "lets fool eveyone'

Its actually cleverly written and the plot moves rather swiftly. Outside of the initial misgivings about why he has to do horrible things aside, i rather much enjoyed it. The character and background was colorful and the setting wasn't too bad. Still its very much brain candy, but oh how much i savored it right after a long plane ride. =)
