#  There Is No Antimemetics Division
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8649978370)

This was a fun little read from a source material that's mostly looked upon as quirky kooks. Basically the entire book is based from the SCP foundation which, in a nutshell, pretends that the stupid myths from each society (like fan death, or walking under ladders, or broken mirrors) are real and caused by some sort of mystical or alien origin and that a government body has been created to prevent greater catastrophe. Just google SCP Foundation to see some of the more creative takes on it. Seen from a creative fiction point of view its pretty good, seen from a "some folks actually believe this shit" take, its...uh..well the less we say about modern society, the better.

Anyhow, the book is a serious take on the stuff that happens in the SCP Foundation and follows one such agent in one such government body. Its your basic adventure story with an agent who's largely lost her memory, but still have the skills to find and capture/contain the anomalies. There's a greater story than just capturing your everyday anomaly and the author is very good at making the pace of the story go pretty quick. The sense of dread and foreboding that pervades the whole book masterfully captures what a hellhole of a universe where the SCP universe is real and not just imaginations of your everyday superstitious folks.

The pacing of the book is pretty fast and there's very rarely a moment for peace for both the characters or the reader. I really enjoyed the writing and hope the author comes up with another book. I'm not usually a fan of the SCP stuff (the last game I played that was of this concept was Control, and its a great game but the SCP stuff rarely stands up to scrutiny (nor is it meant to)), but this book really takes it to a good enough level that I can tolerate it.

Highly recommended! The content is really well written even if you don't like the source material.
