#  I Want to Die but I Want to Eat Tteokbokki
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8027104828)

I'm pretty sure this book was recommended to me by some algorithm when I had finished "Crying in Hmart" or something, and the title looked interesting enough for me to start the book. I wanted to stop the book soon after, but given that it was short and sunk cost fallacy yadda yadda yadda, decided to finish it.

This book is literally just a transcript of what the authoress talks about to her therapist. full stop. there's a little bit of a "reaction blurb" that happens after the transcript, but there's no plot, no planning, no..nothing.

The authoress sees a therapist, talks about her depression, her fears, her issues..and her therapist responds. The therapist responses ARE interesting because you don't expect a therapist to....be as unhelpful as he or she is. I'm not a therapist and even I know telling your patient "don't be angry" when they're angry bout something is as useful as telling an itching person "don't scratch".

In any case, i'm taking back one star and saying DO NOT READ AT ALL. you'd be angry at the authoress, the therapist and this whole genre of books after this. really. DO NOT READ.
