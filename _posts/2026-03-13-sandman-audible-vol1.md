# The Sandman: Audible Production Vol 1
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8355944601)

This is the second time I've listened to the Sandman audiobook, and as befits tradition, i'm relistening only becuase I wanted to share with my daughter what good literature sounds like. I don't know how many times i've read the comics, but all said and done, I wouldn't be surprised if this is my 10th iteration of readin/listening to the series.

I'm not sure if there's anything left to be said about the Audiobook series that hasn't been already said. The audiobook brings a completely new and awesome addition to whats already a very strong literary series. Neil gaiman rewrote the series to suit the audiobooks and the best additions are translating the visuals of a comic book series to an auditory format. Things that were previously glanced over is now narrated (by gaiman himself!) into rich exposition. This a a fully casted series, so each and every character gets a distinct voices, from the repeating ones, like Morpheus and the other endless, to the characters that only show up in the one and done short stories.

The dailogue was already one of the highlight of the comics, so having the right actors and actresses fully voicing them out is a treat. Its even encouraged my daughter to take a look at some of the one shot stories.

About the only thing i don't fully understand is why the stories happen in a different order as presented in the comics, but this is more of a "i wonder why" and not even a nit.

If you're one of the handful of folks who hasn't yet experienced sandman in any of its iterations, I can wholeheartedly recommend it and say its time you will not regret spending. Even my teenage daughter was entranced by it (and now can somewhat recognize good liteature vs bad literature ;p)
