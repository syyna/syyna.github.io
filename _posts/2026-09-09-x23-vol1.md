# X-23: Vol 1
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8933962272)

Given how smitten I was with the rest of Majorie Liu's other comics (Monstress, Night Eaters), I thought I'd fall in love with X-23 as well.

Unfortunately, what I got was a fairly formulaic female wolverine book. Partially its because I think I'm following the story halfway through. Its way after X-23 got rescued, and is supposed to be part of a team. At the same time, given that this is Vol. 1, I'd have thought I'd get at least a "to catch you up to date" summary. So a lot of my discontent is probably that I had no idea who the characters she's supposed to be part of the team was, why they're all so cold to her, and why she's already suffering from both PTSD and massive team (or maybe teen) paranoia.

In any case, nothing happens of note in this volume. I'm sure I could read the rest to get more out of it, but compared to Monstress or her other works (even her novel!) this was pretty...weak.

Skip unless you already know the backstory. The authoress was either constrained by Marvel, but just assumed everyone knew the story to date.
