# Preaching to the Choir
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8962268971)

This was a bit out of left field type of short story. In many ways, it reminds me of the movie Ready or Not, in that a girl was brought on by his fiancee to "meet the family". Only that the family is not quite what it seems. Its a bit of a mystery novel that has a slight heel turn into the horror, and tries to maintain its mystery feel.

Its a very short read, so even if its not quite your style, I think its still worth the read. You do have to remember that Tchaikovsky started his career with a whole host of insect based books, so perhaps this is just him getting back to its root.

Mild recommend. Not Tchaikovsky's best works, but still worth a read if you're a fan of his.
