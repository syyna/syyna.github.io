# Stardust
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8929474313)

Honestly, if you wanted me to retell Stardust from the movie, I probably couldn't do it very well. It was a movie I watched quite a while ago, and while the movie was interesting, it never captured my attention very much so.

Seeing as how I was already on a Gaiman kick, I figured I might as well read Stardust as well. And much like the movie, if you had to have me describe what it was, I'd say it was partially a comedy and partially a boy's adventure to win a woman's heart. The premise at least starts out that way. This might actually be Gaiman's most romantic novel now that I think about it. A good hearted boy gets seduced, gets married (not to the woman that seduced him), gives birth to a child, and that child goes on to have his own magical adventure. He tries to win the heart of a woman by going forth to find a fallen star, and in the midst of it, find that the star is of herself, a woman as well.

The venture together and goes through many trials and tribulations, and in the end triumph against all odds and live happily ever after.

There is tension, there is villians, and trickery, but of all the Gaiman books I've read, there wasn't the sense of danger. Even coraline, a book for children, has quite a bit of danger and mystery and yes, adventure. It seems that the danger in Stardust was swapped out fro romance instead.

As such, I have to say that while the book was very well done, it might be the most...plain... of all the gaiman books that I've recently read.

Still well done, hence the 4 stars, but if you have to pick one gaiman book to read for the rest of your life, this probably wouldn't be it.
