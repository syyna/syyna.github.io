# Astrophysics for People in a Hurry
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8596607831)

This is one of those books that you want to say you read and understood. But honestly, unless you're an astrophysics, you probably will not understand even a tenth of what the book has to say. I've got a decent science and math background and even I was glaze-eyed by the end of chapter 2.

The books rating and my own subsequent "good feels" about the book is more to do with how beautiful Neil deGrasse Tyson narrates his own text than the concepts of the text itself. Science books have this problem. Humans intuitively only remember narratives, and science has no narratives. Science just is. This means its very hard to make a science book especially memorable to your audience if all you want to do is stick to the science.

As the quote says "The universe is under no obligation to make sense to you". I'd follow it up with the allegory that "The universe is also under no obligation to ENTERTAIN you".

The book is entertaining and you WILL be educated while you are reading this, but how much you retain? I'd be surprised if you can remember more than the many quips in the book.

Still, might be worthwhile just to listen to Tyson narrate. It keeps its high rating for me for that fact alone.
