# Anansi Boys
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8908277564)

Right on the heels of finishing American Gods, I jumped straight into Anansi Boys because that's the "sequel" of sorts. Anansi was the spider god from American Gods, and one of the more entertaining ones, and reading more about him seemed interesting. And it was, but it took a while to get the hang of the book.

First of all, the book isn't about Anansi, not directly, its about his Boys, as the title alludes to....and the one you meet at the beginning is the "loser" boy for lack of a better word. There's nothing to suggest that he's the son of a god, and you do realize that he IS the son of a god for all the description given about the boy's father. And then, as all things does, Gaiman takes you on this journey where you go down the trials that his character goes though. The father anansi dies, and the son has to deal with the consequences of that. One of which is that he is told that he's got a twin brother that he's never met!

He wishes to meet him, and his wish is granted. Only that his brother is everything that he's not. Smarter, cooler, more fit, more handsome, great with ladies, talented. And slowly starts to take over his life, first his job (albeit only for a day), then his fiance, then his apartment....He ends up trying to find help to get rid of his brother and the hijinks that ensues is the best part of the book.

Like most of Gaiman's book, its a slow burn, so you have to be patient and get through the overly long prologue. Once you're past that part however, its all golden. Highly recommended if you loved American Gods. No signs of Shadow here however, or any of the norse pantheon, but still highly entertaining.
