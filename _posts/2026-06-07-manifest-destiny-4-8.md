#  Manifest Destiny Vol 4 - 8
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8668745560)

This review kinda covers from the middle of the series to the end. The protagonists have finally made it to California and are at the end of the journey. More mysterious happenings happens to the crew and the captains. The antagonists are always hinted upon but never really made clear who they are (it seems like a version of the devil) and the resolution between Louis, Clark, Sacagawea and the french colonists are finally resolved. Things have been building up to a crescendo where you know someone has to die and since you know the 3 main characters live (though they could have always been cloned and replaced or something), who was going to perish was kind of never in doubt. It was just how.

I have to say the ending of the series was fairly satisfactory, although how the showdown between the big bad and the main characters leave a bit to be desired. Still, life is pretty hard to predict and I guess this is relatively realistic. I'd have to say that the series still toes a good line between the horror of war,the horror of exploration and the horror of never having a good safe place.

All in all, I don't mind having read the series that the ending was a bit more of a letdown than I'd have liked. The conceit of the series is still very clever and the execution was quite good more often than not. Endings are notoriously difficult to nail (even the Sandman had a bit of a lackluster ending), and I shouldn't deduct more than a star for that.

Highly recommended comic book series, especially if you want a more legendy feel for the Louis & Clark expeditions.
