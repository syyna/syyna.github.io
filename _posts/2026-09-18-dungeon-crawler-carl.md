# Dungeon Crawler Carl
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8931421197)

This was a book that was recommended to me by AI as I was trying to figure out which Audible only book I could use a credit on. Given that unless I really love the author, I'd never blow a credit on a book that was also available on Libby, and that this was a super long series (8 books at the time I read it), I decided to not blow a credit on it and go back to reading it as an Ebook instead.

The first few chapters were very...cringe. It starts off quite a bit like hitch hiker's guide to the galaxy, with an alien armada basically bulldozing the earth. in this case, instead of a literal bulldozer, its more like everything got piledrivered. everyone who was in a building or a vehicle apparently got crushed and dead. only folks who were outdoors got to survive, and the reward for this survival is that they get to particpate in a dungeon crawl that'll be televised throughout the galaxy. Basically a reality show.

Our titular character and his cat happened to be outside when this event happens and they wander into the dungeon. The rules are that if they can make it past 15 or 16 levels, they can save the entire planet from being made part of the galactic empire or what not.

So the first book is mostly about world building, and setting up the rules that govern how the game is played. Because its a mashup of MMORPGs and Reality TV, the entire first third of the book was..pretty cringey and hard for me to get through. But the writing is crisp and the action moves quick. And then the meat of this first book is how the character and cat survives. They meet up with their "trainer" for lack of a better word, and they get instructions, they get used to their interface and the cat evolves into a player character of their own right (otherwise, pets are just NPCs).

I think this is one of those weird books that shouldn't work, but becuase of the work that the author put into it, somehow magically becomes something more than what it is. There is a hint of an undertone that this is not just a tounge in cheek book, which I would have just finished the first and stopped if that was all there is to it.

If i had to describe what i feel about the book (and the series) is that its one of those novels you read in episodes on the web that then gets packaged into a novel of its own. This READS like that, but unlike those novels, there's going to be a real ending to this one since its good for only at most 15 or so.

Anyway, enough words from me to say...if you like MMORPGs and a reality tv type show mashed into one, interspersed with some hitch hiker's guide, and some really decent writing, then you should try the book. If the letters MMORPG makes no sense to you, or you hate reality TV or you're one of the 5 people who never got Douglass Adam's sense of humor...then hard skip.

The first book is just world buildling, but it does get significantly deeper in the later books.

Recommended if you're the target audience. =)
