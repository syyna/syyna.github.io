#  True Grit
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8667484143)

I watched the Cohen Brother's True Grit with my daughter a few weeks earlier (it was a movie I've been saving to watch together with her for about 10 years), and as she was leaving for Taiwan, told her to read this book with me. Its a pretty short book, and I've been told the Cohen Brother's version was much the same as that of the book, so was interested to see what differences there were and how the story progressed.

Well, the book was excellent. The movie does track the book pretty accurately, with the changes mostly for artistic reason and doesn't materially change the plot. The book does explain a few things more so than the movie does, such as when the indian kids were kicked off the ledge (they had been torturing some animals, which wasn't clear at all in the movie. The interactions between LeBouef and the Marshall were all on point...what you do get is more information about the motivations of the Marshall and his history with the gang and the murderers they were hunting down.

The book is curtly written, and the diction they used was masterfully re-enacted in the Cohen brother's take of the book. Truly I think if you only have time for one and not the other, just watch the movie. The movie's direction and mood takes on the book so beautifully that I think if you only have time for one, you should just watch it. The soundtrack is also superbly well matched to the period.

If this sounds like I liked the movie more than the book, it might be so. This is certainly a case where the movie at least matched the book if not exceeded it. Still the source material is excellent and definitely worth a 2 hour or so read.
