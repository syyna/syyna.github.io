# Manifest Destiy Vol 1 - 4
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8600122512)

How I got into this series is that there was an amazon sale on a game flavor based on this series. I've never heard of either the game nor this series and ultimately the game did not look interesting enough even on a sale basis for me. The comic that the game was based off though...that was interesting enough.

Basically, this is a retelling of the Lewis & Clark expedition with of course, Sacajawea as their guide. It takes a pretty well known story and adds on the fantastic. Its a simpler retelling of tales unlike Fables, but was compelling enough for me to actually read and finish the whole series.

This review covers the first 4 volumes of the book, and its mostly setting the scene. You learn of the REAL reason why Lewis and Clark has to go explore the west and its nothing like what the history book tells you. There's these monsters out in the uncharted territories and Lewis and Clark has to take a squad of soldiers and convicts into this hinterland to find the root of where the monsters are coming from, and if possible, destroy the root.

As usual, do not think too hard about the implications of why send a small scouting mission to destroy what are essentially monsters...if the full might or the US army was to be turned onto this task, it'd be better known as "Invasion of the West" or "Trails (and highways) of tears" and be a much less interesting book, I guess.

In any case there is a new monster(s) of the week per trade paperback volume and always a new challenge for the invaders to overcome. They almost always overcome the challenges but often at great cost. I'd describe the book as both horror and expansionist in nature and given that I never really learned of Lewis & Clark in grade school, this makes learning of it in comics all that much more enjoyable.

Highly recommended. Very entertaining, just don't, uh tell your teacher that Sacajawea fought monsters and destroyed a St Louis Arch lookalike in every state.
