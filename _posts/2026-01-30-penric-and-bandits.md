# Penric and the Bandit
[Goodreads Review] (https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8315620150)

I've read almost all of the 5 world's series written by Lois McMaster Bujold and so when I found Penric and the Bandit, I jumped in joy. Only to find out somehow in the last few years i've missed not one but TWO novellas in the time.

Time truly flies when you're old.

This was a nice short story about Penric, desdemona and their adventures to uncover some lost works by another of the Bastard's order. The mcguffin in other words. Alongside his journey, he meets up with a fellow traveller, who was going to rob Penric until Penric tells him of a temple with even greater treasures.

Penric of course already knows about the traveller's intention, but as gentle as he is, sees it only as a means to cultivate and redeem his fellow traveller. Other hijinks ensure like the traveller's old companions showing up to get their dues, and Penric and Desdemona doing all sorts of things to prevent those companions from getting their way.

All in all, a wonderful return to the World of the Five Gods for me and I didn't begrude a single minute of reading it. Bujold can still spin a wonderful tale with the best of them, and although I didn't like the short novella form of these Penric novels, have to admit that they are great for me where I am in my life now.

I still try to reread Curse of Chalion every so often to remind me of what truly great storytelling is like.

Highly recommended if you love Bujold's Five Gods world, but you'd have already read it by now wouldn't you?
