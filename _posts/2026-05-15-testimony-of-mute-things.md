# The Testimony of Mute Things
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8600117402)

Slowly catching up on the Penric saga. This one is a murder mystery in the agatha christie theme. You have penric going off to figure out some border dispute with his Princess-ArchDivine and then there's this murder of someone who needed to talk to her about some other matter.

Of course, where there's a murder mystery, Penric and Desdemona is going to be there. This is already not the first murder mystery that has to be solved, so they're a bit of an old hands at this. The mystery and motives are all there, and the cleverly covered up crime would have been unsolved were it not for some demonic eyes. Alas for the evil doers, those demonic eyes are for the benefit of the temple and the evil doers are caught.

That'd be a pretty bland tale, but as usual, Bujold manages to spin the humanity into these tales, so you get the idea of what happens to orphans, what happens to older priestesses of the various orders, and the such.

go into this book not for the murder mystery, but for how the tale is spun. The Penric saga is mostly that of a kinder demon and shorter tales, so enjoy this one for the bite sized view of the world you get.
