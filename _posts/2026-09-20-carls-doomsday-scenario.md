# Carl's Doomsday Scenario
[Goodreads Review](https://www.goodreads.com/review/show/8955432239)

Book 2 of Dungeon Crawler Carl. They're now on a new level, even more humans have died, and Carl and his entourage is starting to level up. In this one, they have to perform more quests than just killing something or someone, and the stakes are a little higher.

I think this book does more setup (or the author just decided to turn some unfinished subplots into more plots), and you get propelled from one scenario to another. Overall, its still a page turner even if the details are fuzzy after you've read it. More characters gets fleshed out and you star to see the bigger picture of more than just "reality tv".

I think this is going to be true of the whole series, in that its very much popcorn reading, but its very fun popcorn reading.

If you enjoyed the first book, I cannot see why you'd skip this one, so yah, don't read this one in and of itself, start with book 1!
